# SmartRecipe Usability Test Questionnaires

All items use a 7-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 4 = Neutral, 7 = Strongly Agree). Reverse-scored items are transformed as `7 - raw score` before aggregation.

| P2 | The information displayed on the interface does not make me feel overloaded | 界面展示的信息不会让我感到信息过载 | No |

## Scoring Rules

- Section composite score (one participant) = arithmetic mean of all items in that section, after reverse transformation where applicable.
- Group-level mean = average of composite scores across all 8 participants.
- Relative improvement = (MeanScore_exp - MeanScore_baseline) / MeanScore_baseline.
